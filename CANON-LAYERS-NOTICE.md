# Canon layered editions

Two independent arrangements are offered. Their keys and time maps must not be mixed.

- **Canon in D · Mutopia D-major full score**, 57 measures, quarter note 55 BPM,
  248.727 seconds. Johann Pachelbel, adapted from Mutopia Project #2047,
  typesetting © 2015 Michael Fischer v. Mollard, CC BY 4.0.
  <https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=2047>
  Easy/Normal/Hard are cumulative playable reductions with precisely complementary
  sampled-piano accompaniment. Extreme has all 1,930 distinct physical note attacks
  and no automatic accompaniment in Play Along or Practice.
- **Canon in D (Short ver)**, the existing F-major practice arrangement, approximately
  163.264 seconds (the title preserves the familiar 2:43.2 display).
  The 192-attack Easy remains unchanged. Normal/Hard/Extreme extend that user-provided
  practice chart with reviewed local transcription candidates (258/395/440 attacks),
  not a claim to the official or source-complete OnlinePianist arrangement.
  Shared attacks use Easy's timing/gate first; omitted notes in each level are played
  once by the complementary sampled-piano track. No original recorded backing,
  captured proprietary samples, weights or local song library are added by this release.

The piano samples and their attribution remain in the existing
`PUBLIC-SAMPLE-LICENSES.txt` and `SAMPLE-NOTICE.txt`. They are independent of score rights.
Canonical source hashes and attack counts are in `canon-layer-sources.json`.

Playback:

- Demo = sampled player part + sampled complementary accompaniment.
- Play Along = only the complementary accompaniment automatically; input plays player keys.
- Practice = Play Along plus the existing stop/retry helper and unchanged timing/scoring rules.
- Changing a song or difficulty stops the previous voices and resets the selected chart.

The grand-staff reader at `staff/` provides the separate editions, PC noteheads,
4/8/12/15-system views and each edition's four-curve KPS report.

## Für Elise

**Für Elise · A-minor full written form**, Ludwig van Beethoven, Mutopia #931,
typeset by Stelios Samelis (2015), from Breitkopf & Härtel (1888), Public Domain.
<https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=931>

The complete supplied 905-attack MIDI form is used at quarter = 72 in 3/8. A quarter
of initial rest aligns the pickup, producing 105 notated bars and 131.25 seconds.
No additional repeat passes absent from the supplied source are inserted.
Four cumulative playable charts use 387 / 634 / 787 / 905 attacks, with exact
complements of 518 / 271 / 118 / 0. Extreme is this complete identified written
form, not an audio transcription or a claim to every performance's repeat policy.

This song automatically shows an 88-key A0–C8 keyboard so all actual notes A1–E7
are present without octave folding. Its physical codes are identical to the
grand-staff edition, extending unused keys for low/high pitches. Special keys
(arrows, Home/End, Page Up/Down, Insert/Delete) require the existing full-key
performance focus or may be played using the on-screen keys.
