Nocturne Virtual Piano — public sample credits and release scope

Only the 170 explicitly audited sample files listed for 101 enabled presets are added in this release.
Eight other preset positions are retained but unavailable, without audio assets, bank URLs or substitution.
Displayed generic names are not claims about the instrument manufacturer; recording attribution is retained below.

C5/YDP selected seven roots, 22050 Hz PCM16, fixed layer, maximum seven seconds plus fade, nearest-root browser playback and gain normalization. Audio remains CC BY 3.0; SFZ CC0 does not relicense recordings.
Benjamin Gleitzman MIDI.js Soundfonts conversion. FluidR3 GM by Frank Wen CC BY 3.0 US; Musyng Kite/FatBoy CC BY-SA 3.0. Preserve licenses for sounds/adaptations; software MIT separate.

Existing score and backing files remain byte-identical to public main cb377b81c6e5c912c3d98060ba00d133cf87d372.
Their previous provenance has NOT been re-cleared by this sound-library release. No new OnlinePianist backing recording is uploaded.
