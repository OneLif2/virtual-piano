Nocturne Virtual Piano — instrument sample credits

The 95 instrument choices use 93 distinct public General MIDI banks from
Benjamin Gleitzman's MIDI.js Soundfonts project. Two piano alias choices and
the FluidR3 acoustic grand share one bank. “Concert Grand Piano” and “Grand
Piano 1” use this public replacement; neither contains OnlinePianist's custom
piano recordings. The labels do not imply endorsement.

Pre-rendered Ogg/MP3 MIDI.js conversion: Benjamin Gleitzman's MIDI.js
Soundfonts. Nocturne fetches pinned, unmodified upstream data on demand,
checks the recorded byte count and SHA-256, parses only its note/audio-data
map (never executes the fetched JavaScript), then decodes the selected samples
locally for playback with a gain envelope. Nocturne does not assert ownership
of the recordings. The above credit, terms and applicable share-alike license
remain attached to those sounds and adaptations of those sounds. The separate
MIT license for upstream software is not a replacement for the sample licenses.

If a sample cannot be loaded or verified, the interface identifies the
fallback explicitly: the existing locally synthesized instrument is used,
not a substitute recording silently described as the named sample.
